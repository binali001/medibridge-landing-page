FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is MediBridge?

MediBridge is an early-stage HealthTech platform being developed around a secure digital Health ID and authorized access to relevant patient health information.

Is MediBridge available yet?

MediBridge is currently in research, validation, and MVP development.

Who is MediBridge for?

MediBridge is designed for patients and healthcare providers across participating healthcare facilities.

How does the MediBridge Health ID work?

The Health ID is being designed as a secure digital identity that can help associate relevant health information with a patient.

Can I use MediBridge at any hospital?

Not yet. MediBridge is currently being developed and is intended to work across participating healthcare facilities.

How is my information protected?

Privacy and security are being considered as part of the product design. The final technical and regulatory implementation is still being developed.
